Turning waste into economic value.

Consumers

Return used packaging, receive refundable deposits, earn reward credits, track recycling history.

Brands and manufacturers

Packaging recovery partnerships, collection network access, recovery analytics, traceability and reporting.

Retailers and institutions

Host smart collection stations, improve collection infrastructure, join sustainability programs.

Recycling partners

Receive collected packaging, process recyclable materials, support the circular supply chain.

The deposit is a refundable consumer liability. It is not automatically BUCKSBIN revenue.

BUCKSBIN revenue streams

  1. Smart station installation and deployment
  2. Maintenance and operational contracts
  3. SaaS subscriptions for analytics dashboards
  4. Brand packaging recovery partnerships
  5. Recycling material recovery revenue
  6. EPR-related collection and traceability services, subject to applicable regulations and authorization
